# CatBoxMRE v0.4.0

## NPC quests

Four persistent quests are attached to recurring NPCs:

1. Mimi / Green Hills: earn 6 stars -> unlock Calico.
2. Tiko / Moving Skies: discover the Crusher Vault secret -> unlock Sky.
3. Luma / Ghost Garden: defeat the Phantom Keeper -> unlock Phantom.
4. Mimi / Cat Tower: recover all four secret gems -> unlock Golden.

Talk to the quest giver again after meeting the requirement to claim the skin.

## Wardrobe

Main Menu -> Wardrobe.

Use 4/6 to cycle unlocked skins and 5 to equip. The selected skin is saved in `catbox.sav`.

## Boss phase 2

Every boss changes pattern at half HP:

- Moss King: faster leaps and patrol.
- Picnic Brute: faster, longer charges.
- Phantom Keeper: faster dive/recovery and wider hover.
- Sky Warden: faster aerial orbit and dash.

## Cutscenes

Boss encounters now have three lightweight procedural cutscenes:

- INTRO when entering the arena.
- PHASE 2 when the HP threshold is crossed.
- VICTORY after the boss is defeated.

Press 5 after a short delay to skip.
